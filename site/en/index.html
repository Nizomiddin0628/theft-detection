Hushyor AI — artificial intelligence for store cameras

Hushyor AI connects to the cameras already in your store and analyses the video on site. It sends suspicious moments to Telegram with a photo and a short clip, counts visitors, measures checkout queues and sends a report every morning.

How it works

Video is analysed on the computer in your store and never leaves it. Only a photo, a short clip of each event and totals go to Telegram and the cloud dashboard.

  1. Your existing IP cameras or DVR/NVR are connected. The video stream never leaves the store network.

  2. In every frame it finds people and maps a 17-point skeleton; when a hand reaches for something, it also looks for items around it. By default 4 frames a second when things are calm, 8 when something looks suspicious.

  3. A photo and the reason arrive first, then a clip covering 10 seconds before and after the moment. You choose who gets what.

  4. The guard's ✅ or ❌ is stored: the false-alarm rate is shown openly in reports and the behaviour model is retrained on these answers.

  5. In the browser: live cameras, events, visitors, queues, flow and settings. Works without the internet too.

  6. All branches in one place, with roles. Only events, clips and totals go to the cloud; if the internet drops, they wait in a queue.

  7. Every morning: yesterday's numbers and recommendations (PDF too). For critical events such as a fall or a night-time entry — an SMS.

Who it's for

Every store has different problems — settings and zones are chosen to match.

  • Supermarkets and convenience stores

    Checkout queues, peak hours and small-item theft.

  • Clothing and footwear

    Items hidden under clothing, fitting-room areas, movement between sections.

  • Pharmacies and cosmetics

    Small, valuable items, the till area and after-hours entry.

  • Electronics and phone shops

    Lingering at display cases, items going into bags, night-time security.

  • Retail chains

    Each branch runs on its own computer; the owner sees them all in the cloud dashboard.

Privacy and requirements

Privacy

  • No face recognition — the system judges actions, not identities.
  • The video archive stays on the computer in your store.
  • Access is limited by role: owner, manager, guard.
  • A person checks every alert; the false-alarm rate is shown openly in reports.

What you need

Computer
Windows 10 or 11, 8 GB RAM, a 4–6-core processor (recommended). We measure how many cameras it can handle during the site visit.
Cameras
IP cameras with an RTSP stream, or a DVR/NVR.
Internet
Needed for Telegram and the cloud. If it drops, alerts queue up and are delivered once it's back.
Phone
Any phone with Telegram.

Questions

Didn't find your answer? Leave a request and we'll get in touch.

Do I need to buy new cameras?

Usually not: IP cameras with an RTSP stream or a DVR/NVR can be connected. During the visit we tell you which camera suits which job (for queues, for example, a camera that sees the till from above works best).

What happens if the internet goes down?

Analysis runs on the computer in your store, so monitoring continues without the internet. Alerts and numbers reach Telegram and the cloud once the connection is back.

Where is the video stored?

The video archive stays on the computer in your store. Only a short clip and a photo of each alert go to Telegram; if you use the cloud dashboard, alert clips and totals go there too — never a continuous stream.

Does the system make mistakes?

Yes, like any camera system. That's why a person checks every alert, the false-alarm rate is shown openly in reports, and zones and sensitivity are tuned from those numbers.

Does it recognise faces?

No. The system does not identify anyone — it judges actions: hands, bags, lingering at a shelf, queues, crossing a line.

I have several branches — can I see them all in one place?

Yes: the computer in each branch connects to the cloud dashboard. The owner sees every branch; managers and guards see only their own. Every Monday a summary comparing branches arrives.

What if the computer goes off?

The software starts with Windows and restarts itself if it hangs. If a camera goes offline you get a Telegram message; if a branch drops off the network, the cloud dashboard shows it.

What language are the dashboard and bot in?

Uzbek (Latin script) for now. If you need Russian or English, mention it in your request.
